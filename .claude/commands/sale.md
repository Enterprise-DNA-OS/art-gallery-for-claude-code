description: Sale
---

Read the artwork and its agreement. Run `node scripts/gallery.mjs sale "<stock>" --collector="<name>" --ref="<unique invoice reference>" --cents=<gross amount> --due=YYYY-MM-DD --market=AU --resale=true --json`. Use the actual market and resale status. This records a sale, closes offers, and does not create a tax invoice or charge a card. It refuses missing or expired agreements.

Never send, publish, transfer funds or invent records. Drafts and documents remain private.
